Accessibility

Updated November 22, 2026

We want rizobot.com to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Semantic HTML with landmarks, headings in order and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Text and controls meet AA color contrast in both light and dark mode, which follow your system setting.
  • Screenshots and diagrams have text alternatives that describe what they show.
  • Pages work when zoomed to 200% and on small screens, and respect your "reduce motion" setting.
  • No content flashes, autoplays or relies on color alone.

Known limitations

  • Code samples are shown as preformatted text. Screen readers read them line by line, which can be slow for longer samples.
  • Some linked pages, such as GitHub, npm and the VS Code Marketplace, are run by others and have their own accessibility practices.

Report a barrier

If something on the site is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell us at chaitanyaaggarwal9@gmail.com. Include the page and what happened, and we'll reply within 5 business days and work to fix it. We can also send the information you need in another format.

The software

The Rizo extension runs inside VS Code and uses VS Code's own themes, fonts and keyboard handling. If you find an accessibility problem in Rizo, please open an issue.
